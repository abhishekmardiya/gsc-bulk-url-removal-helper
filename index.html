Quick Instructions

  1. Navigate to Google Search Console.
  2. Select your property and go to Removals.
  3. Open the browser console (F12 or Cmd+Opt+J).
  4. Paste the generated script on the right and hit Enter.
  5. Important: Ensure the GSC tab remains active and visible.
